Question 4 - Two approaches for varying browsing fraction at 75% load:

Q4a (Dynamic Lambda): For each browsing fraction, recalculates the bottleneck station 
and uses 75% of that specific lambda_max. This keeps the system at 75% of its capacity 
regardless of the mix, producing a smooth graph.

Q4b (Fixed Lambda): Uses a single lambda_total calculated at 90/10 mix 
(lambda_total = 0.75 * lambda_max_90/10). When the browsing fraction changes, 
the bottleneck shifts but lambda stays fixed, causing saturation at certain mix ratios 
(e.g., 82-85% browsing shows U > 1.0 at Frontend).
